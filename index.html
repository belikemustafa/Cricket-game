📖 Pro Batting & Timing Guide

1. Read the Pitch Ring

Watch the pulsing marker on the pitch: Red signals seam/pace (Yorker, Bouncer, Swing), while Purple warns of sharp Off-Spin turn.

2. Step Across the Crease

Use ← / → (or swipe on mobile) before release to back away for room or step across into the line of wide yorkers.

3. Loft vs Grounded Shots

Press Shift / W to switch between 🚀 LOFT (soaring sixes over the ropes) and ⚡ GROUNDED (safe bullet drives through gaps).

4. Sprint Smart Runs

Tap Space or the red SPC : RUN box while the ball is in the deep to steal singles and twos—just beat the fielder's throw!
